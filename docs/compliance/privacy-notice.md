## What QuantClarity stores about visitors

QuantClarity does not create accounts or profiles and does not store visitor information. A destination provider becomes a separate party when you follow its link and may process information under its own privacy notice. A commission, if any, never affects QuantClarity's facts, coverage, search, ordering, or access.
